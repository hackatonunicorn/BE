EcoTech Solutions - Sustainable Energy Management Platform

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
EcoTech Solutions is a CleanTech startup developing an AI-powered energy management platform for commercial buildings. Our technology reduces energy consumption by 30% and carbon footprint by 40% through intelligent optimization.

PROBLEM
Commercial buildings waste 25-30% of their energy due to inefficient management systems and lack of real-time optimization. This costs businesses billions annually and contributes significantly to carbon emissions.

SOLUTION
Our proprietary IoT sensor network combined with machine learning algorithms provides real-time energy optimization for HVAC, lighting, and electrical systems. The platform learns usage patterns and automatically adjusts settings for maximum efficiency.
